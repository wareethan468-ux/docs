Anti-spam watches how fast a member posts, how often they repeat themselves and how many people they mention. Past your limits, their recent messages are removed and they get a short timeout.

## Flood protection

Zone watches how quickly each member sends messages, repeats the same message, and mentions other people. When they pass your limits, their recent messages are removed and they get a short timeout.

Limits are per server, so a busy community channel can be looser than a quiet one.

## Word and link filters

Add banned words or blocked links to stop them appearing before staff sees them. Filters work alongside flood protection and can be switched on independently.

Roles, channels and whole categories can be exempted — useful for announcement channels and for staff.

## Examples

### Enforce, don’t just watch

```text
/antispam set enabled:true limit:6 window:5 timeout:60 mode:Enforce log_channel:#mod-log
```

Six messages in five seconds, one minute of quiet, and a line in the staff channel.

### Leave staff alone

```text
/antispam exempt role:@Staff
```

Exempted roles and channels never count towards the limit — useful for announcements and bots.

### Avoid — Wrong target type

```text
/antispam exempt user:@member
```

Exemptions are for roles and channels. A single member cannot be exempted, so Discord rejects the member picker here.

<Note>
  Filters match what members type in your server. They do not read direct messages.
</Note>
