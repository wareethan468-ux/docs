Warnings are stored against the member with a reason and the staff name. When someone reaches a count you have set, Zone applies the matching action on its own.

## Keeping a record

A warning is stored against the member with the reason and who gave it. Staff can look up a member’s history at any time, including after they change their nickname.

Warnings survive restarts, so the record is there months later.

## The escalation ladder

You can decide what happens at two warnings, five warnings, or any other count: a timeout, a kick or a ban. Zone applies the rule the moment a member reaches that number.

The ladder is edited in the dashboard under Moderation, and you can remove a rule whenever it stops feeling right.

## Starting over

Clearing a member’s warnings is deliberate — it wipes their history for that server, so use it after an appeal, not as routine tidying.

## Examples

### Record it

```text
!warn @member reason:"Repeated pings"
```

The member sees the warning and the reason, and it stays on their history after a restart.

### Read the history

```text
!warnings user:@member
```

Shows every warning with the reason, who gave it and when.

### Avoid — Clearing everyone

```text
!clearwarnings
```

A member is required — this wipes one history at a time, which is deliberate. There is no accidental server-wide reset.
