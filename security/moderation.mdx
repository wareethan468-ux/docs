Everything here is one action with a reason attached: a timeout for a cooling-off period, a kick to remove someone, a ban to keep them out, and a warning when you want the behaviour on record.

## Start here

- [**Bans, kicks & timeouts**](/security/moderation-actions) — Remove someone for good, for now, or give them a cooling-off period.
- [**Warnings & escalation**](/security/warnings) — Keep a record, and let Zone step in automatically after repeat behaviour.

## Examples

### The first step

```text
!timeout @member duration:10m reason:"Cooling off"
```

They stay in the server and can read, but cannot post until it expires.

### When it is clearly deliberate

```text
!ban @raider delete_messages:Last 24 hours reason:"Raid advertising"
```

Clears a day of their messages at the same time, so the channel is readable again.

### Avoid — No duration

```text
!timeout @member reason:"Cooling off"
```

A timeout without a length would never end, so the duration is required. Use a value like 10m or 2h.
