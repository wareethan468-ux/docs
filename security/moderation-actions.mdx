Timeout keeps the member but mutes them, kick removes them and lets them rejoin, ban keeps them out until you lift it. Every action records the reason you type.

<Warning>
  Zone cannot act on a member whose highest role sits at or above its own. Fix the role order before assuming a command is broken.
</Warning>

## Choosing the right action

A timeout keeps the member in the server but stops them talking for the minutes you set. A kick removes them and they can rejoin with a new invite. A ban stops them returning until you lift it.

Every action records the reason you type. It shows up in the confirmation the member receives and in the staff log, which matters when someone asks why weeks later.

## Clearing messages

Message clean-up removes a batch of recent messages, optionally only the ones from one member. It is the fastest way to deal with a spam burst after the person is timed out.

## Limits you cannot bypass

Nobody can act on a member with an equal or higher role, including Zone. If an action silently does nothing, check who sits above whom in the role list.

## Examples

### Ban and clean up

```text
!ban @raider delete_messages:Last 24 hours reason:"Raid advertising"
```

The reason is stored and shown in the moderation log, not just the reply.

### Remove one person’s messages

```text
!purge amount:50 user:@spammer
```

Leave the member out to clear the last batch regardless of who sent it.

### Avoid — No target

```text
!ban reason:"Raid advertising"
```

The member is required. Zone will not guess who you meant, so nothing is banned.
