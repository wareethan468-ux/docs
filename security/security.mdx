Anti-nuke stops one account damaging the server, anti-spam handles floods, the honeypot catches raiders who wander into a trap channel, and permissions keeps dangerous grants visible.

## Start here

- [**Anti-nuke**](/security/anti-nuke) — Watches for mass deletions, bans and role changes, then stops the account doing them.
- [**Anti-spam**](/security/anti-spam) — Clears spam floods and adds word and link filters on top.
- [**Honeypot**](/security/honeypot) — A trap channel that moderates anyone who posts in it.
- [**Permissions**](/security/permissions) — Grant and remove permissions in bulk, and see who holds the dangerous ones.

## Examples

### A sensible starting threshold

```text
/antinuke set enabled:true limit:3 window:10 log_channel:#mod-log
```

Three destructive actions in ten seconds trips it. Adjust once you know how busy your staff is.

### Flood protection

```text
/antispam set enabled:true limit:6 window:5 timeout:60 mode:Enforce
```

Six messages in five seconds earns a minute of quiet. Alert only logs without acting.

### Avoid — Missing the switch

```text
/antinuke set limit:3 window:10
```

`enabled` is required, so Discord refuses the command. Zone never guesses whether you meant to turn something on.

<Note>
  Security features react to what is happening now. They are not a backup tool — keep your own copy of anything you would hate to lose.
</Note>
