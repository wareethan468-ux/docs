These are separate checks. Satisfying one does not automatically satisfy the others.

## Staff and owner access

Manage Server opens shared setup. Stronger and owner-only actions still apply their own rules. Server administration does not grant bot-owner access.

## Bot access

Zone must see the destination and perform the action there. Channel overrides can deny access even when a server role grants it.

## Role hierarchy

Place Zone above roles it must assign or change. Moderation also checks actor and target order. Managed roles and protected targets have additional restrictions.

## Review access

- Keep sensitive reviews and logs in approved staff channels.
- Test as a normal member.
- Administrator bypasses many private-channel rules; grant it deliberately.
