Discipline & safety groups the workflows below. Timed restrictions, staff diagnostics and member safety.

## What this module does

- Ban a user and retain a permanent re-ban rule
- Restrict a member to a configured jail role
- Restore member roles saved by the jail command
- Assign a role that expires automatically
- Enable or disable stored roles for a member after rejoining
- Server-deafen a member in voice
- Undeafen a member in voice
- List current timed Zone restrictions

## Before you start

Confirm the affected target and your permission.

Check Zone role order.

Depending on the action, this module uses **Ban Members, Manage Roles, Moderate Members, Manage Server, Manage Channels, Manage Messages**. Stronger ownership and channel checks may apply.

## Setup and access

This module has no dedicated entry in shared setup. Use its supported Discord workflows. The command browser provides exact inputs; one-time configuration tools may still exist outside /setup.

## Everyday workflow

1. Review current state.
2. Apply the action matching server policy.
3. Inspect the result and moderation history.

## Check the result

- Roles at or above Zone cannot be managed by it.
- Reports cannot reconstruct unknown historical events.

## Troubleshooting

Check the target, destination and saved settings before repeating a mutation. Use the current browser inputs and the Troubleshooting guide for access, hierarchy and provider issues.
