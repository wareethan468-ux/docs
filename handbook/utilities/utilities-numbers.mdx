Number calculations groups the workflows below. Calculate percentages, factors and number bases.

## What this module does

- Show min, max, sum, count, mean and median
- Calculate what percent one number is of another
- Calculate percentage change from old to new
- Reduce an integer numerator/denominator
- Check whether a positive integer is prime
- List positive factors of an integer
- Convert an integer to binary, octal and hexadecimal
- Convert a number from 1 to 3999 into Roman numerals
