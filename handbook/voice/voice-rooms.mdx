Create personal voice rooms when members join a configured hub.

## What this module does

- Configure temporary voice rooms
- Open controls for your temporary voice room

## Before you start

Choose a hub and room category.

Check channel management, voice movement and access permissions.

Depending on the action, this module uses **Manage Server**. Stronger ownership and channel checks may apply.

## Setup and access

Open /setup, choose **Voice & Music**, then **Voice Master**. Choose an action, fill required settings and run it. Use the linked setup guides for each system.

## Everyday workflow

1. Select the hub/category and enable the module.
2. Optionally post the control board.
3. Join as a member and test room controls.
4. Leave and check empty-room cleanup.

## Check the result

- Occupied rooms are preserved.
- Members control their own tracked rooms.
- The creator and bot retain access needed by controls.

## Troubleshooting

Check the target, destination and saved settings before repeating a mutation. Use the current browser inputs and the Troubleshooting guide for access, hierarchy and provider issues.
