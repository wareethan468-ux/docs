YouTube groups the workflows below. Search, select and page through videos, channels and uploads.

## What this module does

- Look up public YouTube channels, videos, playlists and uploads

## Before you start

Join a voice channel and give Zone Connect and Speak.

Choose an allowed source or search phrase.

Individual actions can still check channel access, ownership and service authorization.

## Setup and access

This module has no dedicated entry in shared setup. Use its supported Discord workflows. The command browser provides exact inputs; one-time configuration tools may still exist outside /setup.

## Everyday workflow

1. Queue a track and confirm playback.
2. Review the queue before adding more.
3. Use controls from the bot voice channel.
4. Stop or leave when finished.

## Check the result

- Providers can fail while the bot is online.
- Source resolution and voice permissions are separate checks.

## Troubleshooting

Check the target, destination and saved settings before repeating a mutation. Use the current browser inputs and the Troubleshooting guide for access, hierarchy and provider issues.
