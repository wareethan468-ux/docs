Post a persistent verification panel that grants a configured member role.

## What this module does

- Post the verification panel and choose its member role
- Summarize recorded website verification outcomes
- Inspect a member’s verification status without revealing IPs

## Before you start

Create a regular member role below Zone.

Give Zone Manage Roles and choose a public panel channel.

Depending on the action, this module uses **Manage Server**. Stronger ownership and channel checks may apply.

## Setup and access

Open /setup, choose **Community**, then **Verification**. Choose an action, fill required settings and run it. Use the linked setup guides for each system.

## Everyday workflow

1. Choose the role and destination.
2. Post the panel and verify as a member without the role.
3. Check access granted by the role and repeat as an already-verified member.

## Check the result

- Managed, missing and uneditable roles do not grant access.
- The role opens only the channels members should reach.

## Troubleshooting

Check the target, destination and saved settings before repeating a mutation. Use the current browser inputs and the Troubleshooting guide for access, hierarchy and provider issues.
