Give members private support channels with claims, access controls and transcripts.

<Warning>Administrator permissions can bypass private channel rules. Give support access only to trusted roles.</Warning>

## What this module does

- Set up the ticket panel
- Manage who can open support tickets
- Show recent tickets
- Ticket panel, stats, reputation, IDs or detailed lookup
- Look up a ticket by its ticket ID
- List ticket IDs you are allowed to view
- Open a support ticket
- Close this ticket

## Before you start

Choose a support category, public panel channel and trusted staff roles.

Give Zone access to the support area and permission to manage its channels.

Depending on the action, this module uses **Manage Server, Moderate Members**. Stronger ownership and channel checks may apply.

## Setup and access

Open /setup, choose **Community**, then **Tickets**. Choose an action, fill required settings and run it. Use the linked setup guides for each system.

## Everyday workflow

1. Save the support roles and destination, then post the panel.
2. Open a ticket as a test member and claim it as staff.
3. Inspect access for the opener, assigned staff and added members.
4. Close the test ticket and download its transcript before deleting its channel.

## Check the result

- The opener can reach the ticket.
- Current staff can use the appropriate controls.
- A failed close or transcript is reported rather than treated as complete.

## Troubleshooting

Check the target, destination and saved settings before repeating a mutation. Use the current browser inputs and the Troubleshooting guide for access, hierarchy and provider issues.
