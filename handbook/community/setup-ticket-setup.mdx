Give members private support channels with claims, access controls and transcripts.

<Warning>Administrator permissions can bypass private channel rules. Give support access only to trusted roles.</Warning>

## Before you start

Choose a support category, public panel channel and trusted staff roles.

Give Zone access to the support area and permission to manage its channels.

Manage Server opens shared setup; action-specific checks still apply.

## Open the setup flow

1. Open /setup in the server being configured.
2. Choose the category and **Tickets** module.
3. Choose the relevant action and edit its settings. Required fields gate Run action.
4. Run the action and read its result. Reopen expired menus when needed.

## Configure and test

1. Save the support roles and destination, then post the panel.
2. Open a ticket as a test member and claim it as staff.
3. Inspect access for the opener, assigned staff and added members.
4. Close the test ticket and download its transcript before deleting its channel.

## Verify your setup

- The opener can reach the ticket.
- Current staff can use the appropriate controls.
- A failed close or transcript is reported rather than treated as complete.

## Maintain the configuration

Keep destination channels and roles current. Replace deleted Discord objects through the matching setting. Disabling, resetting and deleting are different operations; inspect the selected action before applying it.

## Settings

### staff_role

Support role; blank keeps saved roles

### staff_roles

Support roles, separated by commas

### channel

Panel channel; blank keeps the saved channel

### category

Category for new tickets

### notify_role

Role to notify for new tickets

### transcript_channel

Where ticket transcripts are saved; blank keeps the saved channel

### max_open_tickets

Open tickets allowed per member (1–25); blank keeps saved limit

Range: 1 to 25.
