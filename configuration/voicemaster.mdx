A hub channel creates a private room when someone joins it. Inside, the member renames it, caps it, locks it or hands it to someone else without waiting for staff.

## How members use it

Pick a hub channel and a category. When a member joins the hub, Zone creates a private room and moves them into it. They can rename it, limit how many people fit, lock it, or hand it to someone else.

No staff involvement, no waiting for a moderator to notice.

## What staff needs to know

Zone needs to see the hub, move people and manage channels in that category. Empty rooms are removed automatically; rooms with people in them are left alone.

## Examples

### Build the hub

```text
/voicemaster set enabled:true hub:#Join to create category:"Voice Rooms" panel_channel:#voice
```

The hub is a voice channel, the category is where the temporary rooms appear.

### The in-room menu

```text
!voice
```

One dropdown with rename, limit, lock, unlock, allow, block, transfer and claim.

### Avoid — A category where a voice channel belongs

```text
/voicemaster set enabled:true hub:"Voice Rooms"
```

The hub has to be a voice channel members can join. A category cannot be joined, so the setup is refused.
