Claiming tells the rest of the team a ticket is handled. The opener can close their own, staff can close any, and a transcript saves the last thousand messages as plain text first.

## Inside the channel

Any staff member can claim a ticket so the rest of the team knows it is being handled, and release it again if they have to step away.

The member who opened it can close their own ticket. Staff can close any ticket, and the channel is deleted once everyone has what they need.

## Transcripts

A transcript saves the latest thousand messages as plain text before the channel goes away. Download it before deleting anything — a deleted channel cannot be rebuilt.

## From the dashboard

The dashboard lists open tickets for each server, shows a preview of the conversation, and lets you claim or close without opening Discord. It is the quickest way to check whether anyone is waiting.

## Examples

### Block a repeat opener

```text
!ticket-blacklist add user:@member reason:"Abusive"
```

Existing tickets stay open; this only stops new ones.

### See who is blocked

```text
!ticket-blacklist list
```

Paginated, with the reason and who added them.

### Avoid — A role instead of a person

```text
!ticket-blacklist add role:@Members
```

The blacklist is per member. A role would be ambiguous the moment someone joins or leaves it.
