Members press one button and get a private channel that only they and your staff role can see. Staff claim what they are handling, close what they have finished, and keep a transcript before the channel goes away.

<Warning>
  Anyone with the staff role can read every ticket in the server. Pick a role you would trust with private conversations.
</Warning>

## Start here

- [Setting up tickets](/configuration/ticket-setup) — Post the panel once, and choose who on your staff team should see it first.
- [Handling tickets](/configuration/ticket-handling) — Claim, close and keep a transcript — from Discord or from the dashboard.

## Examples

### Post the board

```text
/ticket-setup staff_role:@Support channel:#tickets category:Tickets transcript_channel:#transcripts
```

One card, one button. Every ticket lands in the Tickets category and transcripts are copied to staff.

### Stop repeat openings

```text
!ticket-blacklist add user:@member reason:"Repeated abuse"
```

The blocked member gets a short message instead of a fresh channel.

### Avoid — Everyone is staff

```text
/ticket-setup channel:#tickets staff_role:@everyone
```

Anyone with the staff role can read every ticket. @everyone means the whole server can, which defeats the point of a private channel.
