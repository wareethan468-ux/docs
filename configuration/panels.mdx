## One clear action

Every Zone message is a card: a heading, a short explanation, and the buttons or menus that belong to it. Nothing is hidden behind jargon, and every card explains what happens next.

Buttons belong to the person who opened the card. Someone else pressing them gets a polite message instead of an action.

## When a panel goes stale

Controls expire after a while so an old panel cannot act on outdated settings. If a panel stops responding, run its setup again in that channel — Zone edits the message and everything works as before.

## Examples

### A panel that does one thing

```text
/verify-setup role:@Verified channel:#rules
```

One instruction, one button, and a role at the end of it.

### A library panel

```text
/config-setup channel:#configs manager_role:@Staff
```

Members download files; managers can edit the panel without seeing every other panel.

### Avoid — A channel Zone cannot post in

```text
!config-panel channel:#staff-only
```

If Zone lacks View Channel or Send Messages there, nothing appears. Give it access to the channel first, then repost.
