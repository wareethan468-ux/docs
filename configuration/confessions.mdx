A panel lets members submit text that Zone reposts anonymously under a number. Categories can route serious confessions to staff and lighter ones to a public feed.

## Posting the panel

Place a confession panel in a channel. Members fill in the short form and Zone posts the text anonymously with a number, so nobody can trace it back.

Different categories can send confessions to different channels — serious ones to staff, lighter ones to a public feed.

## Keeping it clean

A per-server word list stops banned phrases before they are posted. Managing confessions needs Manage Server, and the feature is off until you post a panel.

## Examples

### Point it at a channel

```text
!confessions add channel:#confessions
```

The channel is required — that is where posted confessions appear.

### Review blocked words

```text
!confessions blacklist list
```

Anything on this list is stopped before it is posted, so it never reaches the channel.

### Avoid — No channel

```text
!confessions add
```

Without a channel Zone has nowhere to post, so the command is refused rather than guessing.
