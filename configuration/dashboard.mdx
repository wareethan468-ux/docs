Sign in with Discord and the dashboard lists every server you own or help manage, with overview numbers, moderation, tickets, giveaways, logs, configs, panels and staff access.

## Signing in

Sign in with Discord and Zone lists every server you own or help manage. It only asks for the servers you can already see — not your messages, your member list or your direct messages.

Your session stays in your browser. Logging out forgets it completely.

## What you can do there

Each server has an overview with member, ticket and giveaway counts, the invite link, and sections for moderation, tickets, giveaways, logs, verification, config files, panels and staff access.

Anything you change in the dashboard behaves exactly like the Discord action, including the staff checks and role order.

## Who gets in

Owners and anyone with Manage Server always get in. You can also add specific roles or individual accounts as managers without giving them the keys to the whole server.

## Examples

### Getting in

```text
Top bar → Dashboard
```

Available from every page of this site. Sign in once and the browser remembers the session.

### Where the deeper settings live

```text
Sidebar → Moderation → warning ladder
```

The ladder and escalation rules are easier to edit here than in Discord.

### Avoid — Nothing to show

```text
Sign in with an account that manages no servers
```

The dashboard only lists servers you own or have Manage Server on. Invite Zone to one you run and it appears on the next sign-in.
