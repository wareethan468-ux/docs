Pick a staff role, a panel channel and optionally a category, then post. Re-running the setup changes only what you pass and edits the existing panel instead of posting a second one.

## Posting the panel

Choose a staff role, the channel the panel should live in, and optionally a category where new ticket channels should appear. Zone posts a card with a single Open a ticket button.

Each click creates a private channel that only the member and your staff role can see.

## Changing one thing

Run the setup again with only the value you want to change filled in. Everything else keeps its saved setting, and the panel is edited rather than duplicated.

## Keeping the inbox clean

Members can be blocked from opening new tickets, which is the calm version of "we have answered you three times already". Blocked members see a short message instead of a new channel.

## Examples

### Notify the right people

```text
/ticket-setup staff_role:@Support channel:#tickets category:Tickets notify_role:@Support lead
```

A separate notify role can be pinged when a ticket opens without giving it access to every ticket.

### Change one setting

```text
/ticket-setup max_open_tickets:2
```

Everything you leave out keeps its saved value, so only the ticket limit changes here.

### Avoid — A channel where a category belongs

```text
/ticket-setup category:#tickets
```

The category field expects a category, not a text channel. Discord will not accept the channel in that slot.
