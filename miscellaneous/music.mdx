Join a voice channel, ask Zone to play a search or a link, and it builds a queue you can skip, shuffle, loop and reorder. It leaves on its own when nobody is listening.

## When a track fails

A track that cannot be fetched is skipped instead of stalling the queue, and the reason is written to your log channel rather than into the chat.

Zone disconnects when no human listeners remain, or after a couple of minutes with an empty queue — it never plays to an empty channel.

## Playing something

Join a voice channel, then ask Zone to play a search phrase or a YouTube link. It joins you and starts the queue.

The queue view shows what is playing and what is next, with skip, shuffle, loop and volume controls on the same card.

## Limits

Each server keeps up to a hundred waiting tracks. Playlists, live streams and videos longer than three hours are refused.

Zone leaves when nobody is listening, or after a couple of minutes with an empty queue.

## Examples

### Search by name

```text
!music play query:"midnight city"
```

A search phrase or a YouTube link both work; Zone joins your channel if it is not there yet.

### Turn it down

```text
!music volume percent:60
```

Volume is per server and survives track changes.

### Avoid — Out of range

```text
!music volume percent:500
```

Volume is a percentage. Anything above the maximum is rejected instead of being quietly clamped.

<Note>
  Queues are not restored after a restart. If a track cannot be fetched, it is skipped and the reason is written to your log channel.
</Note>
