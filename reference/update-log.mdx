```md
## Zone • Appeals
• Prefix-only `,appeal` family for ban appeals: `enable`/`disable` (start or stop accepting appeals), `form`, `form add/delete <name>`.
• `form remove <name>`, `idlookup <allow|block>`, `invite [<code>]` (no argument generates a never-expiring invite) and `logs <channel>`.
• All rows need Manage Server; the wording matches the panel exactly.
• Intake posting is still to come with the rest of the appeal panel.

## Zone • Counting
• Prefix-only `,counting`: choose a counting channel and starting value with `counting set`, watch the chain in `status`.
• Members count one number per message; a wrong number, a double count or chat deletes the message, breaks the chain and it restarts at one, keeping the best run.
• Staff with Manage Messages or Manage Server bypass the counting channel rules; `reset` and `remove` gate respectively.

## Zone • Filter
• Prefix-only `,filter`: add/remove/list/whitelist/reset filtered words per server, regex patterns and role exemptions.
• Toggles for links (with domain whitelist), emoji threshold, message spam, caps percentage, spoilers and music files, each with `exempt` and `exempt list`.
• `,filter snipe` hides content that matches the filters inside snipe replies.
• `,filter wordmigrate` copies the word list into a Discord Automod keyword rule.

## Zone • Counter
• Prefix-only `,counter`: create public counter channels for members, boosts, channels or roles with a custom emoji.
• `,counter set` binds a counter to an existing channel, `,counter remove` deletes it, `,counter list` shows every counter.
• Counter channel names refresh every minute.

## Zone • Fixes
• `sticker extract`/`emoji extract` failure cards no longer collapse to "Something went wrong." — the reasons now read out directly.

## Zone • Snipe & recovery
• Snipe deleted messages, edits and removed reactions: `!snipe message`, `!snipe edit`, `!snipe reaction`. Bare `!snipe` behaves like `!snipe message`, and `!snipe @member` lists their recently deleted messages across every channel.
• The `message` snipe accepts an optional `user` option that shows that member's recent deletions guild-wide.
• `!reaction history <message>` lists every reaction removed from a message, with jump link and time.
• `!clearsnipe` wipes all cached results for the server; snipe data lives in memory only.
• Staff permission: Manage Messages.

## Zone • Games
• `/game` (slash and prefix): tic-tac-toe and Connect 4 challenges with click boards, leaderboards and per-member stats.
• `!game blacktea start/end` runs a channel tea pot with a Pour button; the top pourer wins at the end.
• `!game juul` steal/hit/pass/flavor/stats/toggle — a server-wide silly item game; staff can toggle it off.
• Game stats persist per server.

## Zone • Spotify
```

```md
## Zone • Confessions
• Prefix-only `!confessions <#channel>` posts a confess panel (Manage Server).
• Members press Confess, fill out a private form and the confession posts anonymously through a webhook with a running number.
• Keep unwanted phrases out with `!confessions blacklist add/remove/clear/list` (Manage Server).
• Categories route confessions to their own channels: `!confessions category add Games #games`, plus `category list` and `category remove`.

## Zone • Brainrot
• Prefix-only `!brainrotlock add @member` brainrots a member: their messages are deleted and reposted as brainrot slang through an anonymous webhook (Manage Messages).
• `!brainrotlock amount 1-5` sets how much brainrot the messages get, `!brainrotlock list` shows locked members and `!brainrotlock remove @member` releases them.
• `!brainrotlock protect` keeps people safe: `protect add @user`, `protect remove @user`, `protect list` — protected members can never be brainrotted.
• `!brainrotlock roulette` randomly brainrots other messages: `roulette on` / `roulette off` start and stop it, `roulette chance 0-100` sets the ceiling percentage and `roulette limit 1-120` caps messages per hour.
• Prefix commands and staff with Manage Messages always stay untouched.

## Zone • Auto responder fixes
• Removing a trigger no longer fires the trigger itself: prefix command invocations (like `!autoresponder remove hello`) never trigger auto replies.
• Multi-word triggers work without quotes: `!autoresponder add how to get config reply:ask in #support` keeps the whole phrase as the trigger, and `trigger:"how to get config"` plus named replies parse cleanly.
```
