Zone replaces a stack of single-purpose bots: security, moderation, tickets, welcomes, music and config sharing. You turn features on one at a time from Discord, and the dashboard mirrors what is running.

## Start here

- [**Security**](/security/security) — Stop raids, spam and destructive changes before staff has to open Discord.
- [**Moderation**](/security/moderation) — Bans, timeouts and a warning history that stays on record.
- [**Tickets**](/configuration/tickets) — A private channel for every member who needs help, with claims and transcripts.
- [**Community**](/configuration/welcome) — Welcomes, giveaways, voice rooms and anonymous confessions.
- [**Music and tools**](/miscellaneous/music) — Music, logs, config libraries and the panels your members use every day.
- [**The dashboard**](/configuration/dashboard) — See your servers in a browser and manage the important parts from one place.

## Examples

### Opens the menu of every area

```text
/help
```

Narrow it down with the category and module options when you know what you are looking for.

### Jump straight to one area

```text
!help moderation
```

Any area name works, so new staff can find the right screen without memorising commands.

### Avoid — Too many arguments

```text
!help moderation ban extra
```

The help command takes a category and a module — nothing else. Discord rejects the extra word before Zone ever sees it.

<Note>
  Everything is off until you turn it on, and nothing here needs code or hosting. Start with **First setup** when you are ready.
</Note>
